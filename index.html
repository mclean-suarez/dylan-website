Ask us →
Can I cancel?+
Yes, you can cancel your membership any time. That ends your one-on-one support and community access. You keep lifetime access to the Learning Academy.
How do I know they’re actually doing it?+
Every module includes a test or practical project. They can’t progress without completing it, and you’re notified if they stall.
How long do they have to finish?+
They can go as fast or slow as you want, and every module is open from day one. There’s no deadline, and they keep lifetime access to the Learning Academy, so they can come back to any module whenever they need it.
How fast will I see results?+
Most people start using AI in their daily work from the first module — you’ll notice the difference before you read a report.
Can new staff join mid-program?+
Yes. New intake starts every month at level 1.
What if we don’t use Claude?+
The skills are tool-agnostic by design — they carry across to ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Grok and whatever comes next.
Can I add more staff later?+
Yes. Monthly intake is limited so each person gets proper support — it’s best to plan ahead, as some months have a waiting list.
Is there support after the program?+
Yes — with a membership, they get one-on-one support, webinars, podcasts, masterclasses & newsletters, designed to keep your team current as AI moves. Membership is separate from the program fee.
