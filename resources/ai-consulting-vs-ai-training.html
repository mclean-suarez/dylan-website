AI consulting vs AI training: which does your business need?

By Dylan Bailey, Claude Certified Associate
Updated 16 November 2026 · 6 min read
The short answer

AI consulting gets experts to plan or build AI for you. AI training teaches your own people to use and build it themselves. Consulting suits custom systems, complex integrations and large organisations. Training suits the everyday work of every team, and the skills stay in your business. Most small and medium businesses get the most from training-led implementation, adding a consultant only for one-off specialist builds.

Choose AI consulting when

  • You need custom software, a customer-facing AI product or deep integration with an older system.
  • The project needs data engineering or machine learning expertise you don’t have in-house.
  • You’re a large organisation that needs an enterprise-wide strategy, governance and change program.

Choose AI training when

  • The time is lost in everyday tasks: reports, emails, quotes, data entry, scheduling and follow-ups.
  • You want AI used across the whole team, not just one system.
  • You want the skills to stay when a consultant or a keen staff member moves on.
  • The tools you need already exist (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) and your people just need to use them properly.

Why the best results usually combine them

Consultants build things. Training builds people who build things. A business with trained staff gets more from any consultant, because the team can spot what’s worth building, use it properly and maintain it. For most small and medium businesses the sensible order is training first, then a consultant for anything genuinely custom.

Questions to ask either kind of provider

  1. What will be running in our business at the end, and who will own it?
  2. What will our staff be able to do themselves afterwards?
  3. How do you measure the result: hours saved, tasks automated, or something else?
  4. What happens when the tools change in six months?
  5. How do you handle our data, privacy and checking AI’s answers?
  6. Can we speak to a business like ours you’ve worked with?

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between AI consulting and AI training?
AI consulting gets experts to plan or build AI systems for you, usually priced per project. AI training teaches your own staff to use AI and build their own workflows, automations and agents, usually priced per person or team. Consulting suits custom and complex systems; training suits everyday work across every team.
Do small businesses need an AI consultant?
Usually not to start. Most of the time savings in a small business come from everyday tasks that trained staff can automate with existing tools like Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot. A consultant makes sense for custom software, complex integrations or specialist data work.
Is AI training worth it for a business?
It is worth it when staff finish it and use AI in their real work afterwards. Look for training built for non-technical people, practice on your own tasks, coverage of automation and AI agents, progress tracking and ongoing support. Measure it by hours saved and tasks automated, not attendance.
Which is cheaper, AI consulting or AI training?
Training usually costs less per result for everyday work, because one program can lift a whole team and the skills keep paying off. Consulting can be better value for a single complex system that your team could not build. Compare cost per outcome, not the headline price.
Can a training company help implement AI?
Some can. Training-led implementation means the provider supports your staff while they build and deploy real automations and AI agents for their roles. Ad On AI works this way, with one-on-one implementation help from Claude Certified experts.
