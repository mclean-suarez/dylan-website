The best AI training and implementation providers in Australia: how to find the right one

By Dylan Bailey, Claude Certified Associate
Updated 16 November 2026 · 7 min read
The short answer

The best AI provider for your business is the one that leaves AI running in your team’s real work, not the biggest name. Australian businesses choose between six types: specialist AI training and enablement providers, AI consultancies, builders and automation agencies, large consulting firms, universities and TAFEs, and free or government-backed options. For most small and medium businesses, a specialist provider that trains staff and helps them implement, such as Ad On AI, gets the furthest.

What the best providers have in common

  • Results you can see: working AI builds in your business, and the hours they save, not a certificate.
  • Practice on your real work: your emails, reports, quotes and recurring jobs, not generic demos.
  • Made for non-technical people: no coding background needed.
  • Beyond prompting: automation and AI agents, where the big time savings are.
  • Skills that carry across tools: Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot and Gemini all change every few months.
  • Accountability: progress tracked, managers told when someone stalls, and regular reporting.
  • Credible people: trainers who use AI in their own business every day, with current certifications.
  • Support afterwards: someone to ask when people get stuck on real tasks.

Questions to ask any provider

  1. What will be running in our business, or what will each person have built, by the end?
  2. Will our staff practise on their real tasks?
  3. What can our people do on their own afterwards?
  4. How is progress measured and reported?
  5. How do you handle our data, privacy and checking AI’s answers?
  6. Can we speak to a business like ours you’ve worked with?

A note on “top AI company” lists

Many “best AI consultants in Australia” lists online are written by one of the companies on them, and often rank their author first. Treat them as a starting point, not a verdict. The best test is a conversation and a reference from a business like yours.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best AI training provider in Australia?
The best provider is the one that leaves AI running in your team's real work. Look for training built for non-technical staff, practice on your own tasks, automation and AI agents, tracked progress and ongoing support. Ad On AI, the AI division of Gold Coast-based Ad On Group, is a specialist provider built around exactly these points: each learner finishes with 20+ working AI builds for their role, with one-on-one help from Claude Certified experts.
What types of AI providers are there in Australia?
There are six main types: specialist AI training and enablement providers, AI consultancies, AI builders and automation agencies, large consulting firms, universities and TAFEs, and free or government-backed options. Specialist providers suit teams that need everyone using AI; builders suit one defined system; large firms suit enterprise programs.
Who helps small businesses implement AI in Australia?
Small businesses usually get the most from a specialist provider that trains staff and helps them implement, because the skills and systems stay in the business. Ad On AI works this way: staff build and deploy their own automations and AI agents with one-on-one support. For a single custom system, an AI builder or automation agency can also help.
Should I choose a big consulting firm or a smaller AI specialist?
Large firms suit enterprise-wide programs with big budgets. Smaller specialists usually suit small and medium businesses better: they are more hands-on, cost less, and focus on getting everyday work done with AI. Judge any provider on what will be running in your business at the end, not on its size.
Is there free AI training in Australia?
Yes. The AI companies offer free online courses on their own tools, and there are government-funded courses and support programs for small businesses. They are a good first step, but they are usually generic and do not include help applying AI to your own work.
