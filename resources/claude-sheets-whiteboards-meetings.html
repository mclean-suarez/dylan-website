How to use Claude Sheets, Whiteboards & Meetings

How to turn them on, what each one does, and the prompts to copy.

By Beau Robards, Certified Claude Expert
Published 12 November 2026 · 7 min read
The short answer

Sheets builds spreadsheets for you. Whiteboards turns a rough plan into a timeline. Meetings joins Google Meet and takes the notes. Sheets and Whiteboards launched in beta on 10 November 2026 on paid Claude plans. Meetings is still rolling out.

SheetsSpreadsheets from a descriptionOut now · beta WhiteboardsPlans into timelines, docs and decksOut now · beta MeetingsNotes and action items from callsRolling out

1. Turn them on

  1. Get a paid plan. Sheets needs Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise. Whiteboards works on any paid plan. They aren't on the Free plan.
  2. Open them. Ask for a sheet or whiteboard in any chat, or pick one from the Artifacts tab. If they're not there yet, wait a few days and refresh. The rollout is gradual.
  3. On Enterprise, an owner switches them on in Organization settings › Artifacts. On Team, they're already on.

2. Claude Sheets

Describe the sheet and paste in your numbers. Claude builds the tabs, formulas, dropdowns and charts. Your team can then edit it together, with or without Claude.

hiring-budget.txt
Build a hiring budget for [2027] as a Claude Sheet.

Roles (title, team, start month, salary):
[Senior designer, Design, Jan, $118,000
Marketing lead, Growth, Feb, $105,000
Ops coordinator, Ops, Mar, $72,000
Sales exec, Sales, Mar, $65,000
Support rep, Support, Apr, $52,000]

Tabs:
- Budget: role, team, start month, salary, super at [12%], cost this year (pro-rated from the start month), and a Status dropdown (Open, Interviewing, Hired)
- Roles: a short description and the must-haves for each role
- Summary: total cost for the year, cost by team, and a bar chart of cost by team

Use formulas for every total so I can check them. Our budget for the year is [$450,000]: show how much is left, in red if it goes negative.

More sheets to try

cash-flow.txt
Build a 12-month cash flow forecast starting [December 2026]. Monthly income: [list]. Recurring costs: [rent $2,400, software $380, wages $9,500]. Start from a bank balance of [$20,000], show the running balance each month, and highlight any month it drops below [$5,000].
quote-calculator.txt
Build a quote calculator for my [cleaning] business. Inputs: [service type dropdown, size in square metres, hours, extras]. Rates: [paste your rates]. Add GST at 10% and a total, and a second tab listing every quote with the date and customer.
campaign-tracker.txt
Build a campaign tracker for [our December promos]: campaign, owner, channel, start date, end date, budget, spent, and a Status dropdown (Planned, Live, Done). Add a Summary tab with spend against budget by channel and a bar chart. Turn anything over budget red.
content-calendar.txt
Build a content calendar for [December]: date, a Platform dropdown (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn), idea, owner, and a Posted checkbox. Add a tab that counts posts per platform with a pie chart.
clean-a-list.txt
I've imported [our customer list]. Clean it up: fix capitalisation, split full names into first and last name, flag duplicate emails, and add a tab counting customers by [suburb].
budget-vs-actual.txt
Add a tab comparing our budget with what we actually spent each month. Here are the actuals: [paste]. Show the difference in dollars and as a percentage, and turn any overspend red.

Tip: Give it real numbers. You can start from a CSV, Excel file or Google Sheet, and export back to .xlsx any time.

3. Claude Whiteboards

Paste a plan the way you'd say it. Claude lays it out as a timeline with owners and dates, then turns it into a doc or a slide deck.

launch-plan.txt
Make a whiteboard for our [spring launch]. Here's the plan as we talked it through:
[Promo ads go live Nov 27, Jess books the shoot. Tom emails the list on Dec 2. Priya has the new landing page live by Dec 8.]

Lay it out as a timeline with each task, its owner and its due date. Put anything without an owner or a date in a "Needs a decision" corner.

When I say it's final, turn it into a one-page doc and a 3-slide deck: the plan, the timeline, and owners and deadlines.

More boards to try

team-retro.txt
Make a retro board for [last month] with three areas: what went well, what didn't, what to try next. I'll share it so the team can add notes. When we're done, group the notes by theme and draft action items for each person.
brainstorm.txt
Make a brainstorm board for [ways to get more Google reviews]. Add 10 starter ideas as notes, then group them by effort and impact so we can see the quick wins.
process-map.txt
Map our [new client onboarding] on a whiteboard, from [first enquiry] to [first invoice paid]. Show who does each step and mark where things usually get stuck: [your notes].
event-plan.txt
Plan our [end-of-year party] on a whiteboard: venue, catering, invites, budget. Work back from [12 December] and give each task an owner and a due date.
content-plan.txt
Lay out next month's content on a whiteboard, week by week: [themes, launches, key dates]. When it's final, turn it into a Claude Sheet with one row per post.

Tip: Drag a box around part of the board and ask Claude about just that section. Boards also export to Miro, Lucid or Vani by Zoho.

4. Claude Meetings

Claude joins your Google Meet call as a visible participant. It drafts the plan while you talk, and leaves notes and action items when you hang up. According to launch reports, it's coming to Team and Enterprise first, and it won't join calls with outside guests unless an admin allows it.

Don't have it yet? Paste your notes or transcript after the call and get the same result:

after-the-call.txt
Here are my notes from today's [spring launch sync]:
[paste notes or transcript]

Write a 3-line summary, a list of action items with an owner and due date for each, and anything we didn't decide. Then make a whiteboard with the timeline.

Before you use it: tell everyone on the call that Claude is taking notes. Recording rules differ between Australian states and territories, so check the ones that apply to you.

Take the timeline on our [spring launch] whiteboard and build a Claude Sheet to track it: one row per task with owner, due date, budget, spent, and a Status dropdown (Not started, In progress, Done). Add a Summary tab with total budget against spent, and highlight anything overdue.

Then write a one-page doc I can send to [our client] summarising the plan and the dates.

6. What they can't do yet

Not yet
  • Pivot tables and filter views
  • Find and replace
  • PDF export
  • Editing sheets on mobile
  • Meetings on Zoom or Teams
Not planned for now
  • Macros, VBA or Power Query
  • Live data from your apps in a sheet
  • More than 100,000 cells per sheet

Need one of those? Export to .xlsx and finish it in Excel.

FAQs

Do Claude Sheets and Whiteboards cost extra?
No separate price has been announced. They come with paid Claude plans while in beta. They aren't on the Free plan.
Can I use my existing spreadsheet?
Yes. Start from a CSV, Excel file or Google Sheet, or paste rows in. You can export back to .xlsx or CSV at any time.
Does Claude Sheets replace Excel?
For trackers, budgets and calculators, it can. For pivot tables, macros or very large files, export to .xlsx and use Excel.
Does Claude Meetings record the call?
Anthropic hasn't said whether it keeps a full recording or transcript. Reports say notes and action items are saved in Claude after the call.
Why can't I see Sheets or Whiteboards?
Check you're on a paid plan, then give it a few days and refresh, because the rollout is gradual. On Enterprise, an owner has to switch them on in Organization settings › Artifacts.
Can I use them on my phone?
Whiteboards work on mobile. Sheets can be viewed on mobile, but editing is on web and desktop for now.
