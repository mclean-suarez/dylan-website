Who we train

AI training for Australian businesses

By Beau Robards, Claude Certified Associate
Updated 13 November 2026 · 6 min read
The short answer

Ad On AI trains non-technical staff at Australian businesses to use AI in their everyday work, then to build the automations and AI agents that handle the rest. The program is online and self-paced, so teams can take part from anywhere in Australia, with 1-on-1 help from Claude Certified Associates at Ad On Group’s head office in Burleigh Heads on the Gold Coast.

The training is for small and medium businesses that want the whole team using AI properly, not just one enthusiastic person. Nobody needs a technical background. Everyone works through the same core curriculum and applies each module to their own job, so a bookkeeper and a sales coordinator finish with different builds that both get used every day.

By role

What people in each role typically build during the program:

Finance and accounts

Variance commentary, invoice chasing, reconciliation prep and supplier comparisons.

Operations

Procedures written from a voice note, weekly reports pulled together automatically, job and roster summaries.

Marketing

Campaign performance reviews, content drafts in your brand voice and competitor summaries.

Admin and office

Inbox triage, meeting notes with actions, document formatting and data-entry checks.

Customer service

Reply drafts that follow your policies, enquiry triage and a searchable answers library.

Sales

Lead research, quote and proposal drafts, call notes into the CRM and follow-up sequences.

Executive and virtual assistants

Calendar and travel planning, briefing packs and browser tasks handed to an agent.

Owners and managers

Decision summaries, team reporting and a clear list of what to automate first.

By industry

The program isn’t a different course for each industry. Learners apply every module to their own work, so it fits wherever there is repetitive admin, writing, reporting or follow-up. Examples of what that looks like in common Australian industries:

Accounting and bookkeeping

Client query replies, month-end checklists, BAS prep notes and plain-English report summaries.

Legal

First-draft letters, document summaries, matter checklists and precedent searches (with human review).

Financial services and insurance broking

Meeting notes, file notes, renewal reminders and product comparison summaries.

Real estate and property management

Listing copy, tenant and owner replies, inspection reports and maintenance follow-ups.

Construction and trades

Quotes from site notes, job scheduling, supplier price comparisons and safety paperwork.

Healthcare and allied health (admin)

Booking replies, recall letters, policy documents and reporting, keeping patient data in approved systems.

Recruitment and HR

Job ads, candidate summaries, interview guides and onboarding packs.

Marketing and creative agencies

Campaign reviews, content calendars, client reports and briefs.

Retail and ecommerce

Product descriptions, customer emails, stock and sales summaries and review replies.

Hospitality and tourism

Booking enquiries, menus and listings, staff rosters and review responses.

Logistics and transport

Delivery updates, exception reports, quote requests and route or job summaries.

Not-for-profits

Grant application drafts, newsletters, volunteer communications and board reports.

How it works

  1. A short discovery call about your team, the roles involved and the work that eats the most time.
  2. Your team joins the Ad On AI Academy. Every module is open from day one, with AI built into the platform.
  3. 24 hands-on modules across the essential skills, automation and AI agents. Each one ends in a test or practical project, and managers are notified if someone stalls.
  4. 20+ working builds per learner, plus a documented record of hours reclaimed. Lifetime access to the material, and optional ongoing support.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer AI training in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane?
Yes. Ad On AI's program is delivered online and self-paced in the Ad On AI Academy, so it is available to businesses in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra, Hobart, Darwin and regional Australia. Head office is in Burleigh Heads on the Gold Coast, and 1-on-1 support calls are booked online.
Is the AI training delivered online or in person?
The program is delivered online and is self-paced, so staff can learn around their work from any location. When someone gets stuck they can email a trainer or book a 1-on-1 call from inside any lesson.
Which industries do you train?
The program suits any Australian business with repetitive admin, writing, reporting or follow-up work, including accounting, legal, financial services, real estate, construction and trades, healthcare administration, recruitment, marketing agencies, retail, hospitality, logistics and not-for-profits. Learners apply each module to their own job.
Which roles is the training for?
Non-technical staff in finance, operations, marketing, admin, customer service, sales and executive or virtual assistant roles, plus the owners and managers who lead them. No technical background is needed.
Can you train our offshore or outsourced team?
Yes. The BPO AI Program is a structured, month-by-month track for BPO and outsourced teams, moving staff from novice to advanced AI users with monthly skills checks and progress reporting.
Can staff in different offices do the program together?
Yes. Everyone learns on the same online platform at their own pace, and leadership receives one monthly progress report covering the whole team, wherever each person is based.
