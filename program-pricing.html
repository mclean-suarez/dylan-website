The journey

How each program runs.

Both are self-paced and hands-on. AI Intermediate covers the ground once; AI Enablement goes deeper and on to AI agents.

AI Intermediate · 6 modules
Step 1

Get started

Modules 1–2

Find your way around Claude and set it up so it knows you. Then learn to get good answers, and fix the ones that aren’t.

Step 2

Plug it in

Modules 3–4

Connect Claude to the tools you already use, build a workflow that does a real job, then turn it into a skill.

Step 3

Hand it over

Modules 5–6

Make pages, designs and charts, try other AI tools, then hand Claude whole jobs and put them on a schedule.

AI Enablement · 24 modules
Topic 1

The essential skills

Modules 1–8

Use AI properly: give it clear instructions, check its work, connect it to your email, calendar and files, and set it up so it already knows your work.

Topic 2

Automation

Modules 9–16

Hand over the jobs you repeat every week. Turn them into skills AI runs on demand, then put them on a schedule.

Topic 3

AI agents

Modules 17–24

Let AI handle whole jobs. Plan an agent, build it, launch it, then add more and put a lead agent in charge.

FAQs

The questions we hear most.

Which program should I choose?
If you’re short on time and want Claude doing real work for you quickly, start with AI Intermediate. If you want the full skill set, all the way to building your own AI agents, choose AI Enablement.
Is membership included in the program fee?
No. The program fee covers the modules and the platform. Membership is a separate monthly fee, $100 with AI Intermediate or $200 with AI Enablement, and covers your one-on-one support and the community.
Can I cancel?
Yes, you can cancel your membership any time. That ends your support, community and academy access, and a re‑joining fee applies if you come back later.
Can I transfer my membership?
No. It includes lifetime access to the Ad On AI Academy*, so it can’t be transferred.
How do I book my one-on-one support?
It comes with your membership. Book through the calendar link in your platform, in sessions of at least 1 hour.
Do unused support hours roll over?
No. Support hours are use-it-or-lose-it each month. If you need more, extra support is $300 per hour, ex GST.
What if I don’t use Claude?
AI Intermediate is taught in Claude, so you’ll need a paid Claude plan. AI Enablement’s skills are tool-agnostic by design and carry across to ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Grok and whatever comes next.
Can I add more people later?
Yes. Monthly intake is limited so each person gets proper support. It’s best to plan ahead, as some months have a waiting list.
Is the platform secure?
Yes, all features and content are hosted securely.
When do my entitlements begin?
From the day you start your course.
